# Expeditions

The hero can be on **one expedition at a time**. Pick a region, wait for the timer, and the haul is collected automatically when the hero returns, including while the game is closed.

While away, a lantern walks out from the castle gate toward the region (west or east) and back again.

## Regions

| Region | Base time | Danger | Direction | Loot (before bonuses) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Whisperwood | 20s | 0 | West | 4–7 wood, 0–2 stone |
| Greystone Quarry | 45s | 0 | West | 5–8 stone, 1–3 copper |
| Copperfen Hills | 90s | 1 | East | 5–9 copper, 2–5 tin, 2–4 stone |
| Ironroot Deep | 3m | 2 | East | 5–9 iron, 1–3 tin, 1–2 crystals |
| Shattered Caldera | 6m | 3 | East | 8–14 iron, 4–8 copper, 3–5 crystals |

**Danger** is the armor tier needed to enter. See [Forge](forge.md).

Crystals from expeditions are a random element (Ember, Frost, Verdant or Storm). Early on, this is the only way to get Storm crystals before the mine's Storm Vault is dug.

## Bonuses

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Armor tier | Trips take 8% less time per tier |
| Weapon tier | Wood yield × (1 + 0.25 × tier) |
| Pickaxe tier | Stone and ore yield ×1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5 |
| Quickened Road (spell) | Brings the hero home immediately with the full haul |

Loot ranges on the Explore tab already include these bonuses.
