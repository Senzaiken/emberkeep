# Adventurer's Log

A permanent record of everything the player has ever found or achieved, **across every life**. It lives in the **Legacy** tab (formerly Ascend), under the Dynasty section. The Legacy tab's badge counts new entries since you last looked.

## Lives

Every save starts in **Life 1**. Each ascension starts the next life (Life 2, Life 3, …). The current life is shown next to the Dynasty heading.

## Collectibles

Undiscovered entries are blacked-out silhouettes labelled **???**. Tapping one shows a hint (for example "Somewhere in Whisperwood"). Once found in *any* life, an entry stays found forever. Tapping it shows its details and **"First found in Life N · date, time"**.

| Section | Entries | Unlocked when |
|---|---:|---|
| Folk of the keep | 3 | You meet Arthrex or Brom, or Maren finishes your shipyard |
| Relics | 15 | You find the relic |
| Resources | 9 | You first hold the material or crystal |
| Regions | 5 | Your hero first sets out for it |
| Bestiary | 8 | You defeat that kind of foe |
| Road events | 7 | You first meet the event on the road |
| Arms & armor | 12 | Brom finishes forging that piece |
| Spells | 5 | You learn the spell |
| Crystal Mine | 4 | The gallery is dug |
| Seas & isles | 10 | You sail within sight of the island or castle |
| Your realms | 4 | You claim the castle |
| Ships of the sea | 5 | You sink or board that kind of ship |

## Achievements

Medals in five tiers: bronze, silver, gold, frost and mythic. Locked medals are dimmed but show their requirement, so you know what to aim for. Unlocked ones show the life and time they were earned.

| Group | Tiers |
|---|---|
| Each of the 8 holdings (owned at once) | 5 / 50 / 250 / 1,000 / 5,000 |
| Treasury (gold earned across all lives) | 1K / 1M / 1B / 1T / 1Qa |
| Tapping (taps across all lives) | 100 / 1K / 10K / 100K / 1M |
| Expeditions completed | 1 / 10 / 100 / 500 / 2,500 |
| Foes defeated | 10 / 100 / 1K / 5K / 25K |
| Relics found | 1 / 10 / 50 / 150 / 500 |
| Arthrex's errands completed | 1 / 10 / 50 / 150 / 500 |
| Spells cast (grimoire spells) | 10 / 100 / 1K / 5K / 25K |
| Treasure hunting (island treasures dug) | 1 / 10 / 50 / 250 / 1,000 |
| Sea battles (ships sunk or boarded) | 1 / 10 / 50 / 250 / 1,000 |
| Dynasties (reach life N) | 2 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 100 |

Achievements are checked about once a second.

## Notifications

New entries show a toast ("New in your Adventurer's Log: …", or a count if several arrive together).

## Existing saves

The first time the Log loads on an older save, it fills in what the save already has (relics, resources, spells, gear tiers, mine galleries, regions visited, Arthrex and Brom) as found in **Life 1** at that moment. Achievements already met are awarded right away.
