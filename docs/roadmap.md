# Roadmap

## Next up

- [ ] Decide what day and night mean for gameplay (see ideas below).
- [ ] Tune timings for real play. Expeditions and the mine are short for testing.

## Later

- [ ] **Renown tech tree.** Spend Renown on permanent perks (for example a starting gold pouch, faster forging, extra relic chance, a head start on the mine), on top of the passive +5% gold per point.

- [ ] Premium currency (for example Aether Shards) with an instant full heal as the first use. Hero health is already built to support it.
- [ ] More road events, and region-specific ones for every region.
- [ ] Boss beats at the end of the dangerous regions.

- [ ] Move to Vite + Capacitor and build for iOS via a cloud macOS runner (Codemagic or GitHub Actions) → TestFlight.
- [ ] Cloud save or save export/import, so progress survives switching devices.
- [ ] Sound and music (started from a button, since browsers block autoplay).
- [ ] More hero slots or companions, several expeditions at once.
- [ ] Achievements and a fuller chronicle log.

## Day and night ideas

These are unconfirmed options to choose from:

- Some regions are only reachable at night, or yield more by moonlight.
- Certain crystals mine faster at a given time (Ember by day, Frost by night).
- Holdings shift: farms and markets earn more by day, the tavern at night.
- Spells stronger at a given time, or night-only spells.
- The hero heals faster at night while resting, or foes hit harder at night but drop more.
- Night events: raids, wandering merchants, a comet.

## Open questions

- Should Renown also affect material or crystal yields, or only gold?
- Should the Crystal Mine survive ascension (like gear and spells)?
