# Story and Arthrex

Emberkeep has a light storyline told in dialog cards: a portrait, a name, a line of text, and a **Next** button.

## Opening

On a brand-new save, a short card from the keep sets the scene. The old lord is gone, the keep is yours, and the coffers are empty. It points you to tapping the keep, building holdings, and sending your hero to Whisperwood. Existing saves with progress skip this. **Start from nothing** in the Ascend tab shows it again.

## Guided tutorial

Right after the opening card, a spotlight tutorial starts. The whole screen goes into shadow except the one thing to tap, which gets a pulsing gold outline. A card beside it explains the step. Taps anywhere else are blocked until the step is done.

| Step | Spotlight | Advances when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The keep | You've tapped it 5 times (the card counts down) |
| 2 | The Explore tab | The Explore tab is open |
| 3 | Whisperwood | The hero departs |
| 4 | The Journey view | You tap **Got it** (it explains tapping ahead to help) |

If the tutorial is still showing when the hero returns from their first expedition, it completes itself automatically, before Arthrex appears. Every card has **Skip tutorial**. The tutorial resumes at the same step if the game is closed partway through, and it hides while a story dialog is open. Existing saves with progress never see it. **Start from nothing** shows it again.

## Arthrex, Court Sorcerer

Arthrex has served the keep for longer than your father lived. He lives in the **spire on the east grounds**, which is always standing to the right of the castle. Once you've met him, he can be seen at its door, robed in teal with a crystal-topped staff.

### First meeting

He appears when the hero returns from their **first expedition**. Existing saves meet him after their next trip. Over five pages he:

1. Introduces himself.
2. Explains that magic is bound in four crystals: Ember, Frost, Verdant and Storm.
3. Explains where to find them: the Crystal Mine under the keep (deeper galleries yield more kinds), and loose in Ironroot Deep and the Shattered Caldera.
4. Explains what they're for: Verdant for **Mend**, Storm for **Hasten**, and every spell in the grimoire as reagents.
5. **Gives 10 of each crystal** to get you started.

Afterwards, **Ask about crystals** at the spire replays the explanation.

## The spire

The spire section sits at the top of the **Magic** tab. Tapping the spire in the keep view jumps straight there. A violet **!** floats over Arthrex when he has a new errand or a finished one to hand in, and the Magic tab's badge counts it too. While he's busy between errands, a **⌛ countdown** floats over him instead. The spire section shows "Next errand in …" with a filling bar, and the countdown also appears next to the section heading.

## Errands (quests)

Arthrex offers **one errand at a time**, starting 15 seconds after the first meeting.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Offered | Shows the title, his request and the reward. **Accept errand**, or **Another errand?** (one reroll per offer). |
| Active | A progress bar tracks it. **Abandon** clears it, and the next offer comes 45 seconds later. |
| Done | **Claim reward** (or **Hand over** for supplies). He thanks you with a line of dialog. The next offer comes 90 seconds later. |

Progress only counts from the moment you accept.

### Errand hints

While an errand is active (and not yet finished), the game marks what moves it forward:

| Errand | Where the hint appears |
|---|---|
| Restless Roads | ✦ on the Explore tab, and an "✦ Errand 1/3" tag on that region's row |
| Supplies for the Spire | ✦ on the Explore tab, and a tag showing held / needed on every open region that drops the material |
| Thin the Herds, Old Things Waking | ✦ on the Explore tab, and a tag on every open region |
| A Leaking Roof | A "✦ Errand · tap the keep" pill with progress on the castle scene |

Once the errand is done, the Magic tab's badge and the **!** over Arthrex take over.

### Errand types

"Open regions" are the ones your armor lets you enter. The tier is the number of open regions minus 1.

| Errand | Title | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Walk a region | Restless Roads | Complete expeditions to a random open region: 3 for Whisperwood, 2 for Greystone Quarry, 1 elsewhere. Trips where the hero was beaten back don't count. |
| Slay foes | Thin the Herds | Defeat 3 + 2 × tier foes on the road |
| Bring supplies | Supplies for the Spire | Hand over a material from an open region (wood 25, stone 20, copper 15, tin 8, iron 10, each + 4 × tier). The materials are taken when you claim. |
| Tap gold | A Leaking Roof | Earn gold by tapping, at least 200 or 150 × your tap value |
| Find a relic | Old Things Waking | Find any relic (from tier 1) |

### Rewards

- 1–2 random crystal types, each **2 + tier + (0–2)**. Supply errands pay 2 crystal types with +2 each.
- A 50% chance of gold as well: at least 150, or 60 × tap value + 90 × gold/sec.

## What carries through ascension

Having met Arthrex, and the lifetime counters errands use (trips, foes, tap gold, relics), are kept. Any open errand is cleared.
